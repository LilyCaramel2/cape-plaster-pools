# 💰 1\. Financial and Pricing Policy Essentials

### **Hourly vs Project Rates:** 

Stick to *per-project pricing* based on estimated square meters and materials. For small repairs (under 10 m²), you can charge a **minimum call-out fee** (e.g, R1,500–R2,000) to cover travel and setup.

### Deposits:

Keep your 30% deposit — it’s the standard. Make it *non-refundable once work has started*, but refundable if you haven’t bought materials yet and the client cancels early.

### Payment Schedule:

* **30% deposit** on booking (to buy materials \+ secure the crew)

* **40% mid-job payment** (when the surface or prep work is complete)

* **30% balance** on completion and client sign-off.

### Late Payment Policy:

Include a **7-day payment window for balances**, with a polite 5% late fee after 14 days.  
It deters delays without sounding aggressive.

### Christmas / Builders’ Holiday Pricing:

* South Africa’s **official builders’ shutdown** usually runs from **mid-December (around the 15th) to the 8th of January.**

* Materials are harder to source, crews are limited, and transport is slower — so yes, you can justifiably charge a **10–15% seasonal surcharge** for jobs accepted during that period.

* Phrase it as *“Holiday-rate work incurs a 10% surcharge due to supplier closures and reduced crew availability.”*  
   That sounds professional and transparent.

## **🧱 2\. Operational and Job Management**

### Working Hours:

* Standard: **Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM**

* Saturdays: **Half-day on request** (with surcharge if urgent)

* Sundays and public holidays: **Not standard unless emergency rate applies (1.5×).**

### Weather Policy:

If the job involves outdoor plastering, tiling, or stonework, set a clear rule:

“Work is paused in heavy rain or strong wind to protect the quality of the finish. We resume as soon as surfaces are dry and conditions are safe.”

### Cancellations / Delays:

If a client delays start after deposit, offer one free reschedule within 14 days, then charge R500/day for postponed crew bookings — that covers lost wages.

### Waste & Site Clean-Up:

Add a small “rubble removal” note (R500–R1000 depending on volume) if you’re disposing of heavy waste off-site — this keeps quotes accurate.

## **🧍🏽‍♂️ 3\. Fairness and Crew Management**

### Daily Wages:

* Foreman (Matthew): R3,000/day

* Skilled crew: R900–R1,000/day

* Day labourers (temporary): R750/day

### Equality Policy:

Keep pay consistent across all workers of the same skill level — that’s crucial for morale.  
When possible, provide meals or water on long shifts — small gestures keep trust high.

### Rest and Rotation:

* Limit to **6 working days per week** (Sunday off).

* Mandatory 30-minute break for lunch \+ 2 × 15-minute rest periods.

### Bonuses / Profit Shares:

After good jobs or client compliments, pay a small team bonus (R100–R200 each) — it builds loyalty and accountability fast.

---

## **🛡️ 4\. Security and Property Protection**

### At Job Sites:

* Keep a policy that the foreman (Matthew) stays on-site when materials are present.

* Workers should never be left alone at residential sites.

* No entering private areas of the home without client's permission.

* Tools and materials are removed or locked daily.

**Insurance:**  
If you’re unregistered for now, note this on your quotes:

“Our work is not yet covered under formal contractor’s insurance, but every project is supervised and documented daily for accountability.”  
 Once you’re registered, update this wording to reference public liability coverage.
