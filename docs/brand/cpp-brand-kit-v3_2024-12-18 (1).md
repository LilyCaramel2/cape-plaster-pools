# 🔠 2\. Typography (CANVA BrandKit Settings)

We are standardising on Poppins and Open Sans — two professional, modern, readable font families that work across web, social, and print.

## A. Headings – Poppins

Use Poppins for ALL main headings.

Weights allowed:

* Poppins SemiBold (600) – *Default heading weight*

* Poppins Medium (500) – for sub-headings

* Poppins Regular (400) – for softer sections or descriptions

* NEVER use Poppins Bold (700+) — it looks childish and too heavy.

Styles:

* Sentence case (not ALL CAPS)

* Slightly larger spacing

* Clean, calm, no exclamation marks

* Short, direct lines

Examples:  
 “High-quality finishes for Cape Town homes.”  
 “Expert pool renovation and surface work.”

## 

## B. Body Text – Open Sans

Use Open Sans for all body content, paragraphs, captions, and footnotes.

Weights allowed:

* Open Sans Regular (400) – primary body weight

* Open Sans Medium (500) – emphasis

* Open Sans SemiBold (600) – small sub-section labels

Don’ts:

* No italics for long text

* No thin weights

* No all caps

Open Sans keeps your content readable, clean, and trustworthy.

# 🌊 **Facebook Cover Image Size (2025–2026)**

### **Desktop size:**

**820 × 312 px**

### **Mobile safe area:**

**640 × 360 px**

### **Recommended upload size:**

**1640 × 624 px** (Retina quality)

---

# **⭐ The Important Part → The CROP**

Facebook *always* crops at the **top and bottom**, leaving a “safe area” in the middle.

### **Because of that:**

➡️ You must **keep all text, logos, and important visuals inside the middle 360–400px**.  
 ➡️ Anything outside that will get chopped (especially bottom content).

---

# **⭐ Your question:**

“Chopping about 230px off the bottom gives us the correct size?”

### **✔ YES.**

If you generate your cover image at **1640 × 624 px**, then cropping **\~230px off the bottom** matches the safe zone that Facebook displays on most devices.

Here’s what designers usually do:

### **Generate at: 1640 × 624**

### **Keep the “safe zone” inside: 1640 × \~360**

### **Allow Facebook to crop: \~200–250px from bottom automatically**

This ensures:

* The logo won’t cut off

* The text remains fully visible

* Your brand doesn’t distort

* No parts of your design disappear on mobile
