# **⭐ CAPE PLASTER AND POOLS — BRAND KIT (Copy–Paste for Canva)**

## **1\. BRAND OVERVIEW**

Cape Plaster and Pools delivers premium plastering, resurfacing, pool renovation, and finishing work across Cape Town’s Southern Suburbs, Deep South, and South Peninsula.  
 The brand style is clean, coastal, minimalist, luxury-modern, with a soft aqua palette inspired by ocean water, white render, and warm natural textures.

---

# **2\. BRAND COLOURS (HEX CODES)**

Use these exact colours across all designs.

### **Primary Palette**

* **Deep Ocean Blue** — `#072A42`  
   Main brand colour, used for headers, buttons, nav bars.

* **Aqua Blue / Highlight** — `#41E0FF`  
   Used for accents, icons, button glows, gradients.

* **Soft White** — `#FFFFFF`  
   Clean backgrounds, text contrast.

* **Warm Light Grey** — `#F2F4F7`  
   Section backgrounds, cards, subtle dividers.

### **Secondary Palette**

* **Muted Sand Beige** — `#E8E2D8`  
   Optional warm accent for interior plaster pages.

* **Charcoal** — `#1A1A1A`  
   High-contrast text where needed.

* **Slate Grey** — `#6B7A8F`  
   Secondary text, UI labels.

### **Gradient (Brand Signature)**

Use this for buttons, hero overlays, and backgrounds:

* **Gradient Left → Right:**  
   `#072A42` → `#41E0FF`

---

# **3\. BRAND FONTS**

All fonts must stay clean, modern, and readable, with short, punchy lines.

### **HEADINGS (H1–H4)**

**Font Family:** *Montserrat*  
 **Style:** Bold, uppercase optional, tracking normal  
 **Colour:** `#072A42` (Deep Ocean Blue)

* **H1 – Main Hero Title**  
   Size: **70–90 px**  
   Weight: **700 (Bold)**  
   Line Height: **0.9–1.0**  
   Use: Homepage hero, page titles  
   Vibe: Clean, confident, modern

* **H2 – Section Headings**  
   Size: **40–50 px**  
   Weight: **700**  
   Line Height: **1.1**

* **H3 – Subheadings / Card Titles**  
   Size: **28–32 px**  
   Weight: **600**  
   Line Height: **1.2**

* **H4 – Small Section Labels**  
   Size: **20–22 px**  
   Weight: **600**  
   Line Height: **1.25**

---

# **6\. IMAGE STYLE**

Use bright, modern, blue-white, luxury imagery:

* Clean pools

* Fresh plaster

* White surfaces

* Modern interiors

* Cape Town panoramas

* Real project photos (washed in soft aqua overlays optional)

Avoid:

* Overly warm/yellow tones

* Dark construction photos without correction

* Low-quality/resolution images

---

# **7\. BRAND VOICE**

Short sentences, modern, clean, helpful, Gen-Z-friendly but professional.

Tone:

* Confident

* Minimalist

* Fresh

* Helpful

* Cape Town coastal luxury

Avoid:

* Over-explaining

* Corporate jargon

* Heavy marketing clichés

---

# **8\. LOGO USAGE**

* Primary logo: **Blue logo on white background**

* Secondary logo: **White logo on deep ocean blue**

* Icon-only version: **White or Aqua on dark backgrounds**

Padding: Minimum 24–40 px around all logo edges.

Do not:

* Add shadows to the logo

* Stretch/distort

* Place over busy textures without overlay
