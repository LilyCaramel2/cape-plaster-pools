## **💡 Printing Notes**

* **Size:** Standard 90 × 55 mm

* **Paper:** Matte finish or light satin (gloss looks cheap in this trade)

* **Weight:** 350–400 gsm (sturdy, professional feel)

* **Corners:** Rounded, optional — softer, more modern
