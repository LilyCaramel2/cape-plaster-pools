# Project Overview

# **1\. PROJECT OVERVIEW**

Cape Plaster and Pools is a multi-trade home improvement and renovation company operating across the Deep South and Southern Suburbs of Cape Town, including Noordhoek, Kommetjie, Sun Valley, Capri, Fish Hoek, Muizenberg, Constantia, and surrounding areas.

The website will serve as the central hub for:

* Service information

* Portfolio

* Quote requests

* Calendar scheduling

* Contact and WhatsApp enquiries

* HubSpot CRM and MailerLite lead capture

* AI chatbot communication
